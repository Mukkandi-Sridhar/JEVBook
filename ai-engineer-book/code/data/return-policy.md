# Tarkika Return Policy

## 1. General returns
Most products can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The item must be unused, with all tags, and in its original packaging. Start a return from the Orders page. A courier will collect the item within two working days.

## 2. Electronics
Phones, laptops and tablets can be returned within 7 days, and only for a manufacturing defect. Our service partner must confirm the defect first. Accessories such as chargers and cases follow the general rules in section 1.

## 3. Hygiene products
In-ear audio devices, innerwear and cosmetics cannot be returned once the seal is opened. This protects the next buyer. The rule does not apply if the item arrives damaged or faulty. In that case, see section 4.

## 4. Damaged or faulty items
If an item arrives damaged or stops working within 48 hours, report it from the Orders page with a photo. We will replace it or refund it in full, including items covered by section 3.

## 5. Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method. They reach you 5 to 7 working days after the returned item passes our check. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account you choose.
